# Pebblehertz 0.8.9

- Sleep Schedule uses Android's native 24-hour time picker.
- Duplicate PBW imports are blocked by Pebble UUID.
- Titan 2 battery percentage and charging state are forwarded to PebbleOS.
- Runtime status notifications are removed from the Android shade.
- Notification permission is still requested on Android 13+ for future features.
- The support dialog exposes explicit Wise and PayPal buttons.
