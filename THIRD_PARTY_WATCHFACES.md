## Release policy

Before moving any package to `app/src/main/assets/watchfaces/`, record one of the following:

- an explicit open-source license covering the binary and bundled resources;
- written redistribution permission from the author/rightsholder;
- a reliable source page that explicitly permits redistribution and bundling.

Being downloadable at no cost is not, by itself, proof of redistribution rights.
